Swiss German recording script

Thank you for doing this! Your recordings become a course on her phone, from first words to real-speed conversation, in your voice, the way people really talk here.

Only say the big bold line

  • Say only the Swiss German (the big bold line). The grey lines underneath are just so you know what it means: don't read them out, and don't say the numbers.
  • Say it your way. The bold line is a guess at Bündner German by someone who isn't a native speaker. If you'd say it differently, just say it how you would. Your version is the right one.
  • Normal, relaxed speed, like talking to a friend. Not slow, not "reading out loud" voice.
  • Each line once, then a pause of about 2 seconds before the next.
  • Made a mistake? Pause, and say the line again. The last go is the one that counts.
  • A line nobody here would say? Skip it, or say what people say instead.
  • One audio file per "Recording" block (20 blocks, about 40 lines, roughly 4 minutes each). Name each file with its number. Do as many or as few at a time as you like, and tick them off here (it remembers).
  • Quiet room, phone about 20 cm away, any voice memo app is fine. Send the files whenever (WhatsApp is fine).
